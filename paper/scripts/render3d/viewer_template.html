Interactive 3D comparison of nuclear segmentations

Test volumes chosen by the pre-specified median rule (the volume whose S1 − 2D F1@0.5 is closest to the dataset median; seed 0, ground-truth diameter). Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom; the three panes stay synchronized. Works offline.

Meshes are smoothed surfaces of the evaluated label volumes (final masks exactly as scored, including the rule-T threshold and the removal of fragments smaller than 0.15 of the expected nuclear volume). Error classes are assigned per predicted nucleus: matched (IoU ≥ 0.5 with a ground-truth nucleus), merge (covers more than half of two or more ground-truth nuclei), split (one of several predictions that each cover at least 15% of an unmatched ground-truth nucleus), boundary error (best IoU between 0.1 and 0.5), false positive (otherwise). Missed ground-truth nuclei (best IoU < 0.1) are shown as translucent ghosts. The z axis is shown at its physical aspect ratio.
